\section{Spousal Outcomes around Displacement Event}
This section shows spousal earnings and employment trajectories for displaced versus non-displaced workers' spouses.
\\ The added worker effect (AWE) refers to the labor supply response of spouses
when the primary worker experiences job displacement.
\\ Matching is done exactly on whether the spouse is employed at baseline, and
within cells we also match on spouse baseline log earnings using propensity score matching.
\\
\textbf{Note on zero baseline earnings:} About 40\% of spouses have zero earnings at baseline.
For these spouses, earnings growth ($\frac{y_{i,t} - y_{i,-1}}{y_{i,-1}}$) is undefined.
Log earnings figures condition on positive spouse earnings.
Spouse employment and earnings in levels include all observations.
